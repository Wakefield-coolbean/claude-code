# claude-code

## Pixel Aces

`pixel-aces.html` is a 2D pixel-art jet dogfighting game in the spirit of Bit Planes and Triplane Turmoil. It is a single self-contained HTML file with no external assets: art, terrain, music and sound effects are all generated at runtime. Open it in any modern browser to play.

- **World:** a looping world five screens wide with a smooth, look-ahead scrolling camera, parallax layers and a radar strip.
- **Arenas:** four themes (Emerald Hills, Sunset Canyon, Snowy Peaks, Neon City).
- **Flight model:** you manage the throttle yourself, climbing bleeds speed, diving builds it, and flying too slowly stalls the jet.
- **Weapons:** machine-gun bursts and lock-on missiles with limited ammo.
- **Ejecting:** the pilot parachutes down and can be shot on the way.
- **Jets:** six jets, each with its own ability: afterburner, reflect shield, cloak, EMP, flak burst and flares.
- **Supply crates:** repair, missile refill, rapid fire and ability recharge.
- **Modes:** Death Match, Teams, Duel and Survival, with bots at three difficulty levels.
- **Controls:** local 2-player split screen, gamepads, and touch controls for phones and tablets (including 2P on one tablet).

### Controls

| | Pitch | Throttle | Gun | Missile | Ability | Eject |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 player | ← → (or A D) | ↑ ↓ (or W S) | Space | X | V | C |
| 2P – Player 1 | A D | W S | F | G | H | R |
| 2P – Player 2 | ← → | ↑ ↓ | K / Num0 | L / Num1 | J / Num2 | I / Num3 |

Esc or P pauses, and M toggles the music. The in-game **How to Play** screen covers gamepad and touch controls.
